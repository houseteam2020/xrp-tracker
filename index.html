What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.17 early Saturday, down about 2.4% over 24 hours, about 6.5% lower than 7 days ago and about 15% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 3:22 AM MST; Kraken last trade $1.1679, 24h range $1.1505 to $1.2014). Overnight XRP touched about $1.15 on OKX, its lowest since Aug 20, as more than $16M of leveraged long positions were liquidated Friday (CoinGlass via U.Today, CryptoTicker); no Ripple- or XRP Ledger-specific news was behind the move.
  • The US added 165,000 jobs in October, far above the roughly 59,000 to 65,000 economists expected, and unemployment fell to 4.1% from 4.2%; September was revised up to 60,000 (BLS via CoinDesk, Bitcoin Magazine). Bitcoin barely moved on the release, holding near $75,500 to $75,800, but rate-hike odds for the Fed's Dec 9 meeting rose to about 79% (CME FedWatch via Bitcoin Magazine). Thursday's tech-stock sell-off had already pushed crypto lower.
  • Binance's zero-knowledge proof for its Nov 4 Proof of Reserves snapshot (44 tokens) is ready for user verification (Binance official X, Nov 6). Recovery Update #9 on Nov 6 reopened withdrawals for 58 more network-token pairs, including XRP on Ethereum, with deposits open throughout (Binance via Exchange.news). Binance CEO Richard Teng said it aims to reopen the last six tokens (HYPE, INJ, APT, PLASMA, NEAR, LTC) and all assets on already-restored networks by the end of next week (Binance via The Coin Republic). About 98% of hacked assets have been replenished.
  • US spot XRP ETFs had no net flows on Fri Nov 6, a fourth flat day in a row, after about $530K of net outflows on Mon Nov 2 (SoSoValue via TokenPost, ByKaranteli). Cumulative net inflows are about $1.81B; total net assets fell to about $1.34B as the price dropped.
  • The XRP Ledger's fixCleanup3_4_0 amendment went live Wednesday at 14:52 UTC (7:52 AM MST), at ledger 108,112,129 (XRPLWin via CoinDesk). It closes a flaw that, under certain conditions, let an account authorized only to burn an issuer's tokens also mint new ones. It affected issued tokens such as stablecoins, not XRP itself, and no exploitation has been publicly confirmed (CoinDesk).
  • Uphold launched RLUSD Earn on Nov 5, letting eligible customers earn a variable rate of up to 7% APY on Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin through the Uphold app via Morpho lending curated by Sentora; it is not a bank deposit and is not FDIC insured (Uphold via CCN, Nov 5).
  • crypto.news published a Nov 6 feature on Ripple and C2FO's supplier-financing plan that would settle Instant Pay on the XRP Ledger in RLUSD. Fees and reserves on the ledger still require XRP for operators, but the announced settlement currency is RLUSD, so invoice value alone does not imply proportional XRP demand (crypto.news, Nov 6).
  • On-exchange data: Binance's XRP reserves rose from about 2.35B to about 2.63B between Nov 3 and Nov 5 (about +280M XRP), per CryptoQuant. The source of that increase has not been confirmed by Binance. Posts on Nov 6 to 7 claiming reserves 'dropped to 2.38B' recycle an older Nov 2 reading.
  • Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate in Tuesday's midterm elections (AP via Cointelegraph, Reuters). New members take office in January; analysts quoted by Reuters say a Democratic Congress may slow market-structure bills such as the stalled CLARITY Act.
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph).
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in three new escrows that open in June and July 2029, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold.

LabelMeaning

MainstreamGeneral financial press such as Reuters or Bloomberg.

RumorA claim of fact or future event with no named source.
HighPrimary document, or a newsroom report with named sources or data.

UnverifiedNo corroboration found. Treat as opinion.
ContradictedAvailable evidence points the other way.
