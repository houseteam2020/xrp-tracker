What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.33 Tuesday evening, up about 1.7% over 24 hours, roughly flat (down 0.3%) versus 7 days ago and about 12.3% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 5:57 PM MDT; Kraken last trade $1.3294, 24h range $1.2891 to $1.3329). Bitcoin is back near $80,800, up about 1% on the day, ahead of Wednesday's Fed decision.
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to BNY and regulatory approvals. Reported independently by The Block and Cointelegraph.
  • Ripple and Robinhood partnership: RLUSD is live on Robinhood Chain and Bitstamp by Robinhood and is expected in the Robinhood app later this year; Robinhood will use RLUSD on the XRP Ledger for institutional settlement and adopt Ripple Treasury. No contract value was disclosed. Announced by Ripple Oct 27; reported by Cointelegraph and The Block.
  • Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent spoke with Brad Garlinghouse at Swell on Oct 27, saying dollar-backed stablecoins could strengthen the dollar and add demand for Treasuries (American Banker, CoinDesk). He talked about stablecoins and digital assets broadly, not XRP specifically.
  • Ripple's national trust bank got final OCC approval (announced Oct 26; CoinDesk, Benzinga and others reported it Oct 27). It will manage RLUSD reserves and offer business custody; it cannot take deposits or make loans. Today's deals, like the bank, are mostly about Ripple's stablecoin and services; BNY custody is the one item that directly touches XRP.
Not confirmed: No outlet confirms the '$60 billion in XRP transfers overnight' posts; the closest real figures are an Oct 19 on-ledger volume spike and September futures volume. BNY supporting XRP custody is real, but it is a custody service for institutions, not a purchase of XRP by BNY. Ripple's trust bank is not replacing the Federal Reserve and cannot take deposits or make loans; it is a charter for RLUSD reserves and custody. The CLARITY Act has not been signed into law: the Senate failed to advance it 49 to 50 on Sept 15 and no new vote is scheduled, so 'Trump signs crypto bill' posts are false, and the ones asking you to message them for a wallet guide fit a common scam pattern. The BlackRock MOU announced overnight is with SBI Holdings and Startale about a yen stablecoin; Ripple and XRP are not part of it. GraniteShares' Nov 30 date is for leveraged funds and is the issuer's own filing, not an SEC ruling on XRP. Ripple is not releasing 1.3 billion XRP on Nov 1: the scheduled escrow release is up to 1 billion XRP. No BlackRock XRP ETF filing or BlackRock–Ripple deal exists. Ripple's CEO did not say XRP's market cap will hit '$196 trillion' or price it at $118,920; that post is still being recirculated. Price 'what if' posts ($100, $327,000 and similar) are speculation with no source. Also still unconfirmed: no Bank of America–Ripple partnership, no Trump family quote about XRP. 'Reset is live' countdowns, 'QFS card' help desks, airdrops and move-your-coins messages fit common scam patterns. Details are in the rumors section.
