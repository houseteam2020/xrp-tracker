What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.09 early Monday, down about 6.2% over 24 hours, about 12.9% lower than 7 days ago and about 22% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 3:26 AM MST; Kraken last trade $1.0892, 24h range $1.0807 to $1.1682). It is the lowest level since mid-August. About $32.4M of XRP futures positions were liquidated in 24 hours, about 97% of them longs (Liqwall via The Crypto Times). No Ripple- or XRP Ledger-specific news was behind the move.
  • The drop was market-wide: late Sunday, bitcoin fell about $2,200 in 15 minutes to roughly $73,500 to $73,800, its lowest since August, before bouncing back near $74,000 to $75,000, and ether fell below $2,100 (CoinDesk, Bitcoin.com News, Reuters). More than $1 billion in leveraged crypto positions were liquidated in 24 hours, about $913M of them longs (CoinGlass). Outlets tied the weekend selling to reports involving Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, stalled US-Iran talks, and worries that a Democratic-controlled Congress will toughen crypto rules; none of these is XRP-specific, and no single cause is established.
  • Binance is targeting Fri Nov 13 to reopen withdrawals for its last six paused tokens (HYPE, INJ, APT, PLASMA, NEAR, LTC) and all tokens on already-restored networks; no exact day or hour has been given (Binance Recovery Update #9 via MABOnChain, The Coin Republic). XRP withdrawals on the XRP Ledger reopened Nov 3 and XRP on Ethereum on Nov 6. Binance says about 98% of hacked assets are replenished, and the zero-knowledge proof for its Nov 4 Proof of Reserves snapshot is ready for user verification. Separately, Binance confirmed it will close affected European accounts by Dec 31 after the EU's MiCA transition period expired without it obtaining authorization; deposits are blocked for those accounts but withdrawals remain open (Investing.com, Nov 8).
  • US spot XRP ETFs went the whole week of Nov 2 to 6 without a single day of net inflows: about $530K left Canary's XRPC on Monday, then four flat days (SoSoValue via Bitcoin.com News). Cumulative net inflows are about $1.81B; net assets fell to about $1.34B as the price dropped. For comparison, US spot bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.28B over the same week. Monday's flow data will not be out until this evening.
  • The XRP Ledger's fixCleanup3_4_0 security amendment has been live since Nov 4 (14:52 UTC); it closed a token-permission flaw affecting issued tokens like stablecoins, not native XRP, and no exploitation was reported. The proposed native LendingProtocol amendment was still at 21 of 35 validator votes on Nov 8, with no new approvals since Nov 6, and needs 29 to start its two-week countdown; the related SingleAssetVault amendment has 23 (Coinpaper). Neither is live and no activation date exists.
  • The US added 165,000 jobs in October, far above the roughly 59,000 to 65,000 economists expected, and unemployment fell to 4.1% from 4.2%; September was revised up to 60,000 (BLS via CoinDesk, Bitcoin Magazine). Bitcoin barely moved on the release, holding near $75,500 to $75,800, but rate-hike odds for the Fed's Dec 9 meeting rose to about 79% (CME FedWatch via Bitcoin Magazine). Thursday's tech-stock sell-off had already pushed crypto lower.
  • The XRPL Foundation published a 2027 roadmap listing 24 protocol upgrades (lending, perpetual futures, smart contracts and more), with $5.08M to $5.74M in estimated review and audit costs. Only two are approved; 22 are proposals with no guaranteed launch dates (Coinpaper, Nov 7). An experimental perpetual-futures branch with RLUSD collateral exists but is not an approved upgrade.
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph).
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in three new escrows that open in June and July 2029, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold.

How to read the labels

LabelMeaning
OfficialPublished by Ripple, a partner company, or a regulator about itself.
MainstreamGeneral financial press such as Reuters or Bloomberg.
Crypto pressEstablished crypto newsrooms such as CoinDesk, The Block, CryptoSlate.
CommunityCommentators, influencers and post summaries on X, blogs or video.
RumorA claim of fact or future event with no named source.
HighPrimary document, or a newsroom report with named sources or data.
MediumCredible outlet relaying third-party data or company plans not yet delivered.
LowPartly true, but the conclusion drawn goes beyond the evidence.
UnverifiedNo corroboration found. Treat as opinion.
ContradictedAvailable evidence points the other way.
