What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.31 Monday evening, up about 0.5% over 24 hours and about 0.4% over 7 days, and about 14.3% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 5:57 PM MDT; Kraken last trade $1.3073, 24h range $1.2711 to $1.3100).
  • Ripple's planned national trust bank got final approval: the OCC (the federal bank regulator) granted final approval for Ripple National Trust Bank, completing the conditional approval from December 2025. It will manage the reserves backing Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin and offer custody of digital assets for businesses; it cannot take deposits or make loans, and RLUSD stays issued by Ripple's New York-regulated trust company (Ripple press release via Business Wire, Oct 26, 7:13 PM EDT; announced by CEO Brad Garlinghouse on X). This is about Ripple's stablecoin and custody business, not a change to XRP itself.
  • At Swell, Ripple announced new Ripple Treasury features, including AI workflow tools and '24/7 Returns' that let eligible corporate customers earn returns on qualifying digital assets such as RLUSD outside banking hours (Ripple, Oct 26).
  • Crypto slipped again overnight as traders cut risk before the Fed's Oct 28 decision: bitcoin briefly fell below $80,000 and XRP was down about 3% at one point in Asian hours before steadying near $1.28 to $1.29. A rate hold is widely expected; markets are watching for hints of a December hike, with oil still near yearly highs on US–Iran tensions (CoinDesk and Investing.com, Oct 26).
  • Aviva Investors added a tokenized share class of its £22 billion Sterling Liquidity Fund on the XRP Ledger, following its dollar-fund share class in July. The £22B is the whole fund's size; the two tokenized classes together hold a little over £86 million. These are fund shares, not XRP purchases (Financial News, Oct 26; on-ledger issuance visible on XRPL explorers).
  • XRP-branded ads with the slogan 'Crypto Responsibly' appeared in and on New York subway trains around 42nd Street ahead of Ripple's Swell conference (Oct 27–29); the cost, length and sponsor details have not been officially confirmed (U.Today, Oct 26).
  • US spot XRP ETFs took in about $4.25M net in the week through Oct 23, after a $1.84M outflow the week before ended a 13-week inflow streak; Franklin Templeton's XRPZ led. Cumulative net inflows are about $1.8B and net assets about $1.48B to $1.57B, below inflows because XRP's price has fallen (SoSoValue data via Crypto Briefing and TokenPost). Monday's flow data is not out yet.
  • Franklin Templeton added about $150M of its tokenized money-market fund shares to the XRP Ledger, bringing its XRPL total to about $200M in a single wallet; these are fund shares, not XRP buys (CoinDesk, Oct 24).
  • Ripple's regular monthly escrow release of up to 1 billion XRP is scheduled for Nov 1 (00:00 UTC, which is 6 PM MDT Oct 31); in October 700M was re-locked and 300M stayed out (ledger data via TokenPost). This is routine, not new.
  • Arf, a Swiss-regulated payment-finance firm, partnered with Ripple to provide short-term RLUSD settlement loans on the XRP Ledger; a second phase depends on validator approval of the XLS-65/66 lending amendments (Arf, Ripple, Oct 22).
  • An XRP Ledger bug fix (fixCleanup3_4_0) was pushed from Oct 23 to about Nov 4 after validator support briefly dipped; support is now 31 of 35 (CoinDesk, Oct 22).
  • Evernorth (XRPN) filed a preliminary, not-yet-effective S-1 on Oct 23 for up to 5.2M shares it may sell to Cantor (about $80.7M); the filing does not by itself confirm new XRP purchases (SEC filing).
Not confirmed: Ripple is not releasing 1.3 billion XRP on Nov 1: the scheduled escrow release is up to 1 billion XRP. No BlackRock XRP ETF filing or BlackRock–Ripple deal exists; BlackRock's Max Stein speaking on a Swell panel is not an announcement, and overnight 'BlackRock to sell $27 trillion of XRP' posts have no source.
