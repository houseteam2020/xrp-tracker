What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.06 early Wednesday, down about 2.0% over 24 hours, about 19% lower than 7 days ago and about 21% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 3:22 AM MST; Kraken last trade $1.0613, 24h range $1.0301 to $1.0837). Tuesday's low near $1.03 was the weakest in 60 daily sessions, and the daily RSI momentum gauge is near 24, in oversold territory, which does not by itself signal a bottom (The Crypto Basic, FXStreet). About $21M of XRP positions were liquidated in the 24 hours to 04:20 UTC Wednesday (CoinGlass via The Crypto Times). No Ripple- or XRP Ledger-specific news was behind the move.
  • Tuesday's US inflation report came in hotter than expected: October CPI rose 0.4% (0.3% expected) and core CPI 0.3% (0.2% expected) (BLS via CoinDesk, The Block). Treasury yields jumped (10-year near 4.93%) as traders priced in a Fed hike in December; Fed Governor Waller had floated a possible 50bp move if CPI came in strong (CoinDesk). Bitcoin fell to about $73,000, its weakest since Aug 20, and total crypto market value slipped to about $2.46T, with the Fear and Greed Index at 26 (CoinDesk, CoinMarketCap). Ether, XRP and solana fell more than bitcoin.
  • US spot XRP ETFs had a second straight day of net outflows on Tue Nov 10, about $7.6M, led by Grayscale's GXRP (about $7.2M) and Canary's XRPC (about $450K), after about $10.4M of outflows on Nov 9 (SoSoValue via TokenPost, FXStreet). Cumulative net inflows are about $1.80B and total net assets fell to about $1.19B.
  • Ripple CTO Emeritus David Schwartz said in X posts on Nov 9 and 10 that his holdings are down from a high of about 26M XRP to about 3M, from about 1,500 BTC to 1, and from about 40,000 ETH to 6; he sold about 2.5M XRP in 2025. He cited his own discomfort with risk, said sales are pre-arranged in writing with an attorney at least 30 days ahead, and still holds Ripple stock. He also told frustrated holders there is 'no secret plan' (crypto.news, Coinpedia). These are personal sales over years, not a new Ripple sale.
  • On the XRP Ledger, the proposed native LendingProtocol amendment is unchanged at 22 of 35 trusted-validator votes as of Nov 11 (XRPLHub, XRPScan); it needs 29, held for two weeks, to activate, and SingleAssetVault has 23. Neither is live and no activation date exists. The fixCleanup3_4_0 security amendment has been live since Nov 4. The XRPL Foundation's quantum-resistant key design is a draft in its 2027 roadmap, not live; Ripple's target for a full transition is 2028 (CoinDesk).
  • Binance reopened withdrawals for HYPE and APT (TokenPost, Nov 9) and is targeting about Fri Nov 13 for its remaining paused tokens (INJ, PLASMA, NEAR, LTC) and all tokens on already-restored networks; that is a target, not a guarantee (Binance Recovery Update #9 via MABOnChain). XRP withdrawals on the XRP Ledger reopened Nov 3 and XRP on Ethereum on Nov 6.
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph).
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in three new escrows that open in June and July 2029, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold.

LabelMeaning

RumorA claim of fact or future event with no named source.
HighPrimary document, or a newsroom report with named sources or data.
MediumCredible outlet relaying third-party data or company plans not yet delivered.
LowPartly true, but the conclusion drawn goes beyond the evidence.
UnverifiedNo corroboration found. Treat as opinion.
ContradictedAvailable evidence points the other way.
