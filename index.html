What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $0.99 early Saturday, down about 3.7% over 24 hours, about 15% lower than 7 days ago and about 25% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 3:22 AM MST; $0.9895 last trade on Kraken (Kraken 24h range $0.9775 to $1.0427)). It dipped under $1.00 on Friday for the first time since August without closing below it (Friday's close was about $1.01, CoinGecko), then slipped back under $1.00 overnight (Crypto Times). The 2026 low set in August was about $0.985 to $0.986 (TheStreet, Crypto Times). Analysts cite $0.99 as support and $0.80 as a possible next level if it breaks; that is opinion, not a forecast.
  • Bitcoin fell below $70,000 on Friday for the first time since August and traded near $67,000 to $68,000, below its 200-week average (CoinDesk). CoinDesk tied the drop to a stronger dollar (Dollar Index above 103, highest since April 2025), CME FedWatch odds of about 88% for a quarter-point Fed hike in December after hot inflation data, the 10-year Treasury yield near 5.13%, and Nvidia's CEO turmoil. Crypto liquidations topped $1.6B in 24 hours, nearly 90% of them long positions (CoinGlass via CoinDesk). US spot bitcoin ETFs are now about $1.56B in net outflows for 2026 (SoSoValue via CoinDesk).
  • US spot XRP ETFs had a net outflow of about $1.09M on Fri Nov 13 after two flat days: Franklin's XRPZ took in about $4.45M while Grayscale's GXRP lost about $5.54M (SoSoValue via TokenPost). The last day with net inflows across the group was Oct 29. Cumulative net inflows are about $1.79B and net assets about $1.15B.
  • On the XRP Ledger, the proposed native lending amendment has 22 of 35 trusted-validator votes, up from 19 in mid-October, and the SingleAssetVault amendment it depends on has 23; each needs 29 held for two straight weeks, so neither is live and there is no activation date (CoinDesk, Nov 12). The design offers fixed-term loans with no borrower collateral, relying on off-chain underwriting by loan brokers. Separately, XRPL developers will reset Testnet and Devnet on Nov 25, wiping test accounts; the main XRP Ledger is not affected (CryptoPotato).
  • XRP Ledger unique senders, a measure of active accounts, rose to about 41,700, roughly 42% above the 30-day average of about 29,300 and a four-month high, though far below the all-time high of about 106,000 (TokenPost, U.Today). Payment volume jumped about 207% to about 1.16B XRP on Nov 9 (XRPScan via TokenPost). Both count activity, including exchange transfers during a sell-off, not buying.
  • Binance has reopened withdrawals on 87 networks, including LTC and NEAR, and says it plans to restore all remaining withdrawal services before next weekend (TokenPost, Nov 13). XRP withdrawals were not among the paused tokens in its latest list.
  • At Ripple's Swell 2026 event, CEO Brad Garlinghouse said Ripple's large XRP holdings do not give it control of the XRP Ledger: Ripple runs one of the 35 validators on the recommended list, and holding XRP carries no voting weight (DailyCoin, Nov 10). Ripple does control the timing of its own escrow releases.
  • Ripple CTO Emeritus David Schwartz said in X posts on Nov 9 and 10 that his holdings are down from a high of about 26M XRP to about 3M, from about 1,500 BTC to 1, and from about 40,000 ETH to 6; he sold about 2.5M XRP in 2025. He cited his own discomfort with risk, said sales are pre-arranged in writing with an attorney at least 30 days ahead, and still holds Ripple stock. He also told frustrated holders there is 'no secret plan' (crypto.news, Coinpedia). These are personal sales over years, not a new Ripple sale.
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph).
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in three new escrows that open in June and July 2029, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold.

How to read the labels

LabelMeaning
OfficialPublished by Ripple, a partner company, or a regulator about itself.
MainstreamGeneral financial press such as Reuters or Bloomberg.
Crypto pressEstablished crypto newsrooms such as CoinDesk, The Block, CryptoSlate.
CommunityCommentators, influencers and post summaries on X, blogs or video.
RumorA claim of fact or future event with no named source.
HighPrimary document, or a newsroom report with named sources or data.
MediumCredible outlet relaying third-party data or company plans not yet delivered.
LowPartly true, but the conclusion drawn goes beyond the evidence.
UnverifiedNo corroboration found. Treat as opinion.
ContradictedAvailable evidence points the other way.
