What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $0.95 early Thanksgiving Thursday, up about 4.2% over 24 hours (CoinGecko; $0.9517 last trade on Kraken). It dipped to about $0.89 on Wednesday before rebounding with the wider market (Kraken 24h range $0.8878 to $0.9626). Wednesday's daily close was about $0.936 (CoinGecko). XRP is still about 3% lower than 7 days ago and about 27% lower than 30 days ago (CoinGecko), and remains below $1, where it was last turned back on Nov 23.
  • Bitcoin is about $60,800 early Thursday, up about 3.2% over 24 hours but about 4.4% lower over 7 days and about 24% lower over 30 days (CoinGecko). It fell to about $57,300 on Wednesday after US data, then recovered above $60,000 as stocks bounced and short positions were liquidated (CoinDesk, Investing.com, Motley Fool). October PCE inflation was 3.8% a year earlier, with core PCE at 3.3%, above the 3.2% forecast, and Q3 GDP growth was revised up to 4.3% from 3.4% (US BEA via CoinDesk and Investing.com). CME FedWatch put the odds of a December rate hike near 98% (Investing.com). US spot bitcoin ETFs have had about $4.94B of November withdrawals through Nov 24 (Bloomberg via Investing.com). The Crypto Fear and Greed Index reads 11, Extreme Fear (Alternative.me).
  • Ripple announced on Nov 25 that Yapı Kredi, one of Türkiye's largest private banks, uses Ripple Custody, deployed on the bank's own premises, to hold digital assets for its Yapı Kredi Kripto trading platform (Ripple press release, Official). This is a custody technology deal; it does not involve the bank buying or holding XRP for Ripple.
  • Order-book trading on the XRP Ledger's built-in exchange rose 26% in Q3 to about 4.51 million XRP a day, but the 10 busiest accounts made up 65% of it, and trading by everyone else fell; the number of accounts trading roughly halved over the year (CoinDesk, using data shared by Evernorth).
  • The XRP Ledger Foundation says the XRP Ledger is the first public, permissionless layer-1 blockchain to have its Architecture and Resiliency principles independently verified under the Global Layer One (GL1) Market Infrastructure Toolkit; CertiK did the verification in a report dated Nov 13 and announced it Nov 19 (XRPL Foundation, CertiK via GlobeNewswire). CertiK verified only those principles, not all ten; this is not a certification, regulatory approval or endorsement, and institutions still do their own due diligence (crypto.news, Crypto Briefing).
  • The XRP Ledger Foundation and VS1 Finance open-sourced a lending reference app on Nov 19 that combines five amendments (Credentials, Permissioned Domains, Single Asset Vault, Lending Protocol, Batch) on Devnet, the developer test network; native lending is still not live on the main ledger because the lending amendments have not reached the validator threshold (crypto.news).
  • Evernorth (Nasdaq: XRPN), the Ripple-backed XRP treasury company, reported cumulative write-downs of about $505M on its pre-listing XRP, carrying 346 million XRP at $342M at the end of September, nearly 60% below its original recorded value (about $2.44 per XRP on average) (CoinDesk, Nov 17, from its first quarterly report filed Nov 13). This is an accounting impairment that its rules do not let it reverse when prices recover; it does not mean Evernorth sold XRP. XRPN closed Monday down 5%.
  • US spot XRP ETFs took in about $4.07M on Wednesday, Nov 25, all into Franklin Templeton's XRPZ: their first net inflow day of November and the first since late October, and XRP was the only one of 10 crypto ETF categories SoSoValue tracks with inflows that day (SoSoValue via U.Today and FXStreet). The funds are still about $29M net negative for November, and cumulative net inflows since launch are about $1.79B (SoSoValue via CoinCentral and U.Today).
  • Ripple's Nov 16 Swell 2026 recap says the company expects to exit 2026 approaching a $900M annualized product revenue run rate with more than 1,700 institutions using Ripple (Ripple, Official; company figures, not audited). RLUSD supply was about $2.83B on Nov 20, up about 17% in a month (DefiLlama via Bitcoin.com News), and Binance is running a promotion from Nov 20 to Dec 18 that shares a 600,000 XRP pool among users holding RLUSD on Binance (Bitcoin.com News). RLUSD growth and exchange promotions are not the same thing as demand for XRP.
  • On the XRP Ledger, the Single Asset Vault amendment (XLS-65) gained the required validator majority on the evening of Nov 25, with XRPScan counting 29 of 35 validators in favor (28 needed). If that support holds for two straight weeks, it would switch on no earlier than the evening of Dec 9; it is not live yet. The Lending Protocol (XLS-66) has 28 votes but no recorded majority (Cryptoticker, citing XRPScan). Separately, lender Two Prime said on Nov 18 it now accepts XRP, ETH and SOL as collateral for eligible institutional borrowers; XRP-specific terms were not disclosed (Business Wire).
  • Ripple's Oct 27 partnerships still stand but are mostly plans: BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody, while direct RLUSD mint/redeem at BNY is subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph). Robinhood plans to use RLUSD on the XRP Ledger for institutional settlement through Bitstamp and to add RLUSD to its app later in 2026, with no start date or volume announced (Ripple, CryptoSlate). Separately, RLUSD's circulating supply reached a record of about $2.87B, passing World Liberty Financial's USD1 (Crypto Briefing, Nov 23); RLUSD growth is not the same thing as demand for XRP.
  • At Ripple's Swell 2026 event, CEO Brad Garlinghouse said Ripple's large XRP holdings do not give it control of the XRP Ledger: Ripple runs one of the 35 validators on the recommended list, and holding XRP carries no voting weight (DailyCoin, Nov 10). Ripple CTO Emeritus David Schwartz disclosed on Nov 9 and 10 that his own holdings are down from about 26M XRP to about 3M over years of pre-arranged sales (crypto.news); that is a personal disclosure, not a Ripple sale.
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in new escrows, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold. The next release is due Dec 1.

How to read the labels

LabelMeaning
OfficialPublished by Ripple, a partner company, or a regulator about itself.
MainstreamGeneral financial press such as Reuters or Bloomberg.
Crypto pressEstablished crypto newsrooms such as CoinDesk, The Block, CryptoSlate.
CommunityCommentators, influencers and post summaries on X, blogs or video.
RumorA claim of fact or future event with no named source.
HighPrimary document, or a newsroom report with named sources or data.
MediumCredible outlet relaying third-party data or company plans not yet delivered.
LowPartly true, but the conclusion drawn goes beyond the evidence.
UnverifiedNo corroboration found. Treat as opinion.
ContradictedAvailable evidence points the other way.
