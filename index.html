What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.29 Sunday evening, down about 0.4% over 24 hours and about 0.9% over 7 days, and about 17.5% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 5:57 PM MDT; Kraken last trade $1.2953, 24h range $1.2840 to $1.3138).
  • Crypto sold off broadly on Saturday as the US evacuated personnel across the Middle East over Iranian threats; bitcoin fell to about $79,150, its lowest in over a month, and XRP, ether and solana dropped 3% to 4% at the low before recovering. Bitcoin steadied near $80,400 on Sunday with Brent crude above $117, ahead of the Fed's Oct 27–28 meeting (CoinDesk Oct 24; Investing.com Oct 25).
  • More than $800 million in leveraged crypto positions were liquidated over 24 hours, mostly bullish bets, as thin weekend trading amplified the drop (Coinglass via Investing.com, Oct 25). This tracker covers spot holdings only.
  • US spot XRP ETFs took in about $4.3M net from Oct 19 to 22 and had no net flows on Oct 23; cumulative net inflows are about $1.80B and net assets about $1.48B, below inflows because XRP's price has fallen since much of that money came in (SoSoValue via TokenPost and 24/7 Wall St., Oct 24). No ETF trading happens on weekends.
  • Franklin Templeton added about $150M of its tokenized money-market fund shares to the XRP Ledger, bringing its XRPL total to about $200M in a single wallet; these are fund shares, not XRP buys (CoinDesk, Oct 24).
  • Ripple's regular monthly escrow release of up to 1 billion XRP is scheduled for Nov 1 (00:00 UTC, which is 6 PM MDT Oct 31); in October 700M was re-locked and 300M stayed out (ledger data via TokenPost). This is routine, not new.
  • BlackRock's Max Stein is listed on an invite-only Swell panel about how asset managers build digital-asset strategies (Oct 27); a speaking slot is not a product announcement, and BlackRock has not filed for an XRP ETF (Swell agenda screenshots via Times Tabloid).
  • Mastercard, an official partner of the XRPL Commons hackathon in New York (Oct 24–25), reportedly praised Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin during partner pitches; no Mastercard product or XRP Ledger integration was announced (U.Today, Oct 25).
  • Arf, a Swiss-regulated payment-finance firm, partnered with Ripple to provide short-term RLUSD settlement loans on the XRP Ledger; a second phase depends on validator approval of the XLS-65/66 lending amendments (Arf, Ripple, Oct 22).
  • An XRP Ledger bug fix (fixCleanup3_4_0) was pushed from Oct 23 to about Nov 4 after validator support briefly dipped; support is now 31 of 35 (CoinDesk, Oct 22).
  • Ripple filed an Oct 20 letter backing the SEC's proposed crypto-asset rules and asking for clarity on when tokens stop being securities. The rules are not final and the letter does not change XRP's status (Crypto Briefing; Yahoo Finance).
  • Evernorth (XRPN) filed a preliminary, not-yet-effective S-1 on Oct 23 for up to 5.2M shares it may sell to Cantor (about $80.7M); the filing does not by itself confirm new XRP purchases (SEC filing).
Not confirmed: Ripple is not releasing 1.3 billion XRP on Nov 1: the scheduled escrow release is up to 1 billion XRP (about $1.3 billion in dollar terms at today's price). No BlackRock XRP ETF filing or BlackRock–Ripple deal exists; BlackRock's Max Stein speaking on a Swell panel is not an announcement. Ripple has not signed a '$10 billion' partner; that figure is Arf's annual settlement volume, and viral '$45.50' targets have no basis. Evernorth's Ripple, Kraken, Coinbase and BitGo execution partners were announced Oct 14 and are being reposted as 'just in'. Also still unconfirmed from earlier: no Bank of America–Ripple partnership, no Trump family quote about XRP, and Ripple's bank charter is still only a preliminary conditional approval. 'Ripple × Xaman airdrop' posts and 'QFS' or move-your-coins messages fit common scam patterns; Xaman says it does not run airdrops. New tonight: 'XRP powers the BIS unified ledger' is false (a BIS research prototype only), and Mastercard's praise of RLUSD at a hackathon is not a partnership or product. Details are in the rumors section.

How to read the labels

LabelMeaning
OfficialPublished by Ripple, a partner company, or a regulator about itself.
MainstreamGeneral financial press such as Reuters or Bloomberg.
Crypto pressEstablished crypto newsrooms such as CoinDesk, The Block, CryptoSlate.
CommunityCommentators, influencers and post summaries on X, blogs or video.
RumorA claim of fact or future event with no named source.
HighPrimary document, or a newsroom report with named sources or data.
MediumCredible outlet relaying third-party data or company plans not yet delivered.
LowPartly true, but the conclusion drawn goes beyond the evidence.
UnverifiedNo corroboration found. Treat as opinion.
ContradictedAvailable evidence points the other way.
