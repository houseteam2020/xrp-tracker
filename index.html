What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.28 early Saturday, down about 3.5% over 24 hours and 1.2% over 7 days, and about 16.8% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot; Kraken last trade $1.2755).
  • US spot XRP ETFs had no net flows on Oct 23, ending a two-day inflow run ($0.71M on Oct 21, $3.55M on Oct 22); cumulative net inflows are about $1.81B (SoSoValue via TokenPost).
  • Franklin Templeton added about $150M of its tokenized money-market fund shares to the XRP Ledger, bringing its XRPL total to about $200M in a single wallet; these are fund shares, not XRP buys (CoinDesk, Oct 24).
  • CryptEscrow announced a partnership with Ripple for crypto-funded real estate closings; no financial terms or XRP-specific details were given (CryptEscrow press release, Oct 22).
  • Binance started a campaign on Oct 23 sharing 600,000 XRP among eligible RLUSD holders through Nov 20; US users are excluded (Binance).
  • Arf, a Swiss-regulated payment-finance firm, partnered with Ripple to provide short-term RLUSD settlement loans on the XRP Ledger; a second phase depends on validator approval of the XLS-65/66 lending amendments (Arf, Ripple, Oct 22).
  • An XRP Ledger bug fix (fixCleanup3_4_0) was pushed from Oct 23 to about Nov 4 after validator support briefly dipped; support is now 31 of 35 (CoinDesk, Oct 22).
  • Ripple filed an Oct 20 letter backing the SEC's proposed crypto-asset rules and asking for clarity on when tokens stop being securities (Crypto Briefing).
  • Malaysia's and Thailand's central banks joined Singapore's BLOOM stablecoin payments initiative, where Ripple is one of many participants testing RLUSD trade payments on the XRP Ledger (MAS via The Block, Oct 23).
  • Evernorth (XRPN) filed a preliminary, not-yet-effective S-1 on Oct 23 for up to 5.2M shares it may sell to Cantor (about $80.7M); the filing does not by itself confirm new XRP purchases (SEC filing).
Not confirmed: No Bank of America–Ripple partnership was announced (Bank of America's link is an older RippleNet membership and a small ETF holding), no BlackRock–Ripple announcement or BlackRock XRP ETF filing exists (posts asking whether one comes at Swell next week are guesses), no Trump family quote about XRP was found, and Ripple's bank charter is still only a preliminary conditional approval. Posts telling holders to open 'QFS' accounts or move coins to a new wallet fit common scam patterns. A 'Ripple × Xaman anniversary XRP airdrop' is a scam pattern: Xaman says it does not run airdrops. Russia's VTB is not launching XRP trading for retail (Russia's retail list is BTC, ETH and USDT), and the US is not paying its debt with XRP. Details are in the rumors section.
