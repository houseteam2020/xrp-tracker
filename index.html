What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.08 early Tuesday, down about 0.8% over 24 hours, about 15.4% lower than 7 days ago and about 23% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 3:28 AM MST; Kraken last trade $1.0783, 24h range $1.0750 to $1.1157). Monday's low near $1.075 was the weakest in 60 daily sessions, and its daily RSI momentum gauge is near 26, in what analysts call oversold territory, which does not by itself signal a bottom (The Crypto Basic, FXStreet). About $26M of XRP long positions were liquidated on Nov 9 (The Coin Republic). No Ripple- or XRP Ledger-specific news was behind the move.
  • Monday was mixed across markets: US stocks rallied (Nasdaq +2.75%, S&P 500 +2.35%) while crypto lagged, with bitcoin ending the UTC day near $75,250 after a $73,667 low, and ether and XRP down closer to 4% to 5% (CoinDesk, Basis Desk). The Sunday-night flush liquidated more than $1 billion in leveraged crypto positions, mostly longs (CoinGlass via CoinDesk). Reuters tied the weakness to worries that a Democratic-controlled Congress will toughen crypto rules and to the Binance heist; neither is XRP-specific.
  • US spot XRP ETFs had net outflows of about $10.4M on Mon Nov 9, the largest daily outflow in about eight months, after four flat days; Bitwise's XRP fund lost about $9.4M, Canary's XRPC about $811K and 21Shares' TOXR about $213K (SoSoValue via ChainCatcher, TokenPost, FXStreet). Cumulative net inflows are about $1.80B and total net assets fell to about $1.24B.
  • On the XRP Ledger, the proposed native LendingProtocol amendment rose to 22 of 35 trusted-validator votes by Nov 10 (XRPScan, XRPLHub), up from 21; it needs 29, held for two weeks, to activate, and SingleAssetVault has 23. Neither is live and no activation date exists. The fixCleanup3_4_0 security amendment has been live since Nov 4. Separately, the XRPL Foundation's 2027 roadmap (updated Nov 4) includes a draft design for quantum-resistant account keys at no extra fee; it is not live, owners would have to switch keys themselves, and Ripple's target for a full transition is 2028 (CoinDesk, Ripple).
  • Binance is targeting Fri Nov 13 to reopen withdrawals for its last six paused tokens (HYPE, INJ, APT, PLASMA, NEAR, LTC) and all tokens on already-restored networks; no exact day or hour has been given (Binance Recovery Update #9 via MABOnChain, The Coin Republic). XRP withdrawals on the XRP Ledger reopened Nov 3 and XRP on Ethereum on Nov 6. Binance says about 98% of hacked assets are replenished, and the zero-knowledge proof for its Nov 4 Proof of Reserves snapshot is ready for user verification. Separately, Binance confirmed it will close affected European accounts by Dec 31 after the EU's MiCA transition period expired without it obtaining authorization; deposits are blocked for those accounts but withdrawals remain open (Investing.com, Nov 8).
  • The US added 165,000 jobs in October, far above the roughly 59,000 to 65,000 economists expected, and unemployment fell to 4.1% from 4.2%; September was revised up to 60,000 (BLS via CoinDesk, Bitcoin Magazine). Bitcoin barely moved on the release, holding near $75,500 to $75,800, but rate-hike odds for the Fed's Dec 9 meeting rose to about 79% (CME FedWatch via Bitcoin Magazine). Thursday's tech-stock sell-off had already pushed crypto lower.
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph).
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in three new escrows that open in June and July 2029, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold.
