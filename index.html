What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.26 early Friday, down about 2.9% over 24 hours, about 2.9% lower than 7 days ago and about 16% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 3:26 AM MDT; Kraken last trade $1.2550, 24h range $1.1969 to $1.3169). Crypto sold off Thursday evening after Binance disclosed a major hack; CoinDesk reported XRP down as much as 7.4% at the lows, with bitcoin briefly under $77,000.
  • Binance hack: Binance said Oct 29 that a security incident had an estimated impact of about $6.3 billion, with about $3 billion frozen on-chain, and that it will cover all losses from its SAFU emergency fund and company funds (Binance announcement, CNBC, BBC). TRM Labs attributes it to North Korea's TraderTraitor group and says funds moved across ten blockchains including the XRP Ledger. Deposits have resumed; Binance expects withdrawals back within about 48 hours. Binance.US says it was not affected.
  • US data Oct 29: Q3 GDP grew at a 3.4% annual pace (forecast 3.2%); September PCE inflation was 3.6% year over year and core PCE 3.1%, both in line with forecasts but up from August. The Fed held rates at 3.75% to 4.00% on Oct 28 in a 10 to 2 vote with two members wanting a hike, and markets still lean toward a December hike (CoinDesk, Cointelegraph). Coinbase reported Q3 revenue of $1.20B to $1.22B, slightly below estimates, with adjusted EBITDA above estimates (CNBC, CoinDesk).
  • Ripple and Coinbase announced today that RLUSD can now be traded and held in the Coinbase app, Coinbase Exchange and Coinbase Prime, with 1:1 conversion from dollars, and deposits and withdrawals on the XRP Ledger, Base and Ethereum (Ripple, Oct 28). This is about the RLUSD stablecoin, not an XRP listing change.
  • Moody's rated RLUSD A2 long-term and P-1 short-term on Oct 27, citing reserves mostly in short-term US government assets and cash held at BNY, with a 103% coverage target; it also flagged that holders don't yet have a direct claim on reserves (Moody's, Decrypt, The Block). Ripple separately said it is on track for about a $900M annual product revenue run rate (Ripple, Oct 28), and Garlinghouse said on stage, 'We will never give up on XRP' (U.Today).
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to approvals. Reported independently by The Block and Cointelegraph.
  • C2FO and Ripple will bring settlement for C2FO's new Instant Pay product onto the XRP Ledger, using RLUSD, so suppliers paid early get funds in seconds instead of days (Ripple, Oct 27). Settlement is in RLUSD, not XRP.
  • Ripple and Robinhood partnership: RLUSD is live on Robinhood Chain and Bitstamp by Robinhood and is expected in the Robinhood app later in 2026, with no launch date or eligible states announced yet; Robinhood plans institutional settlement in RLUSD on the XRP Ledger, also with no start date (Ripple, Cointelegraph, CryptoSlate Oct 28).
  • Ripple's national trust bank got final OCC approval (announced Oct 26). It will manage RLUSD reserves and offer business custody; it cannot take deposits or make loans. Treasury Secretary Bessent appeared with Garlinghouse at Swell on Oct 27 and spoke about stablecoins and dollar strength broadly, not XRP specifically.
  • US spot XRP ETFs took in about $8.43M on Oct 29, led by Franklin's XRPZ ($5.57M) and Bitwise ($2.86M), after flat days on Oct 27 and 28; cumulative net inflows are about $1.81B and total net assets about $1.47B (SoSoValue via ChainCatcher).
  • Swell wrapped up Oct 29 in New York. Garlinghouse said Ripple will prioritize XRP 'any place it makes sense' while also using RLUSD and fiat, and set a five-year ambition for XRP to become a standard for institutional payment rails; he gave no price target (TokenPost, FXStreet citing Bloomberg). Separately, XRP Ledger developers proposed a fixCleanup3_5_0 upgrade tightening rules for 'restricted' tokens; it is not active on mainnet (CoinDesk).
  • Ripple's regular monthly escrow release of up to 1 billion XRP is scheduled for Nov 1 (6 PM MDT Oct 31). This is routine, not new. An XRP Ledger bug fix (fixCleanup3_4_0) is expected to activate about Nov 4 if validator support holds.

'XRP holders will get paid just for being active on X, starting next week' names no developer and no program and matches engagement-bait patterns. 'Ripple moved 220 million XRP' posts describe on-chain transfers between Ripple-labelled wallets with no stated purpose; internal moves are common before the Nov 1 escrow release and are not a sale.
