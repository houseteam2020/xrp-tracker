What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.25 early Tuesday, up about 0.5% over 24 hours, about 5.6% lower than 7 days ago and about 17% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 3:21 AM MST; Kraken last trade $1.2538, 24h range $1.2336 to $1.2617). Bitcoin was near $76,800 on Monday (CNBC).
  • Binance published the full zero-knowledge proof for its Oct 31 proof-of-reserves snapshot on Monday, Nov 2, so users can check that balances in 10 tokens, including XRP and RLUSD, were backed at least 1:1 at that time; it is not a full audit, and about 5% of the hacked assets (less-liquid tokens) are still being replaced (The Block, Crypto Briefing, Unchained). Phase two of withdrawals began at 04:00 UTC Tuesday (9 PM MST Monday) across nine networks including the XRP Ledger, rolling out pair by pair; another reserve snapshot is planned for Wed Nov 4 (Reuters, Cointelegraph). Net outflows slowed to about $2.57B on Monday from $6.1B on Sunday (DefiLlama via Cointelegraph).
  • Today, Tue Nov 3, is the US midterm election. Democrats are widely expected to win the House, with the Senate less certain (CNBC). Crypto groups have spent about $176M on the races (Reuters). The CLARITY market-structure bill stalled in the Senate in September, and any crypto bill in the next Congress still needs 60 Senate votes (CNBC, CoinDesk).
  • Ripple and Coinbase announced Oct 28 that RLUSD can now be traded and held in the Coinbase app, Coinbase Exchange and Coinbase Prime, with 1:1 conversion from dollars, and deposits and withdrawals on the XRP Ledger, Base and Ethereum (Ripple, Oct 28). This is about the RLUSD stablecoin, not an XRP listing change.
  • Moody's rated RLUSD A2 long-term and P-1 short-term on Oct 27, citing reserves mostly in short-term US government assets and cash held at BNY, with a 103% coverage target; it also flagged that holders don't yet have a direct claim on reserves (Moody's, Decrypt, The Block). Ripple separately said it is on track for about a $900M annual product revenue run rate (Ripple, Oct 28), and Garlinghouse said on stage, 'We will never give up on XRP' (U.Today).
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to approvals. Reported independently by The Block and Cointelegraph.
  • Ripple and Robinhood partnership: RLUSD is live on Robinhood Chain and Bitstamp by Robinhood and is expected in the Robinhood app later in 2026, with no launch date or eligible states announced yet; Robinhood plans institutional settlement in RLUSD on the XRP Ledger, also with no start date (Ripple, Cointelegraph, CryptoSlate Oct 28).
  • Ripple's national trust bank got final OCC approval (announced Oct 26). It will manage RLUSD reserves and offer business custody; it cannot take deposits or make loans. Treasury Secretary Bessent appeared with Garlinghouse at Swell on Oct 27 and spoke about stablecoins and dollar strength broadly, not XRP specifically.
  • US spot XRP ETFs saw about $529,510 in net outflows on Mon Nov 2, after about +$4.3M for the week of Oct 26 to 30 (SoSoValue via crypto.news and TokenPost). Cumulative net inflows are about $1.81B and total net assets about $1.42B. US spot bitcoin ETFs lost nearly $399M the same day.
  • RLUSD supply on the XRP Ledger fell about 28% in one day, from about 982 million to 707 million tokens between Oct 30 and Oct 31 UTC, while RLUSD on Ethereum rose about 5.7% (TokenPost, CryptoSlate citing DefiLlama). The cause is not established; Ripple has rebalanced RLUSD between chains before. A main XRP/RLUSD trading pool on the ledger changed by less than 1%.
  • SEC Chair Paul Atkins said on Oct 30 at the Economic Club of Dallas that 'most crypto assets are not themselves securities,' pointing to the agency's proposed Regulation Crypto Assets, which is still a proposal (24/7 Wall St). Ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse said about half of RLUSD now sits on the XRP Ledger and the rest on Ethereum (TokenPost, Oct 31).
  • Maple Finance's own disclosures show about 225 million XRP pledged as collateral for three stablecoin loans totaling about $151.8M, due Dec 4, Dec 12 and Jan 15. Displayed collateral levels are about 178% to 188%, and no margin call or liquidation is reported; a maturity can end in repayment or refinancing, not necessarily a sale (CryptoSlate, TokenPost).
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1 (6 PM MDT Oct 31), and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in three new escrows that open in June and July 2029, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold. XRP Insights counts about 31.1 billion XRP still in 99 Ripple escrows.
  • An XRP Ledger fix, fixCleanup3_4_0, is set to activate about 7:46 AM MST Wednesday, Nov 4, if support from 33 of 35 tracked validators holds. It closes a loophole where an account allowed only to burn issued tokens (such as stablecoins) could create new ones in some cases; CoinDesk found no record of it being exploited, and XRP's own supply is not affected (CoinDesk, XRPLWin).
