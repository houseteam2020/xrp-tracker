What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $0.89 early Saturday, down about 7.2% over 24 hours (CoinGecko; $0.8911 last trade on Kraken, Kraken 24h range $0.8592 to $0.9674). It closed Friday at about $0.91 (CoinGecko daily close) and is about 32% lower than 30 days ago; it is about 2.5% below the Nov 14 close (the day of last week's 10% drop). Overnight it fell to about $0.87, below last week's low near $0.89, its lowest price since November 2024 (CoinDesk, Bitcoin.com News). About $18M of XRP futures positions were liquidated over 24 hours, roughly 94% of them bets on rising prices, and funding rates turned negative (CoinGlass via CoinDesk). That describes leverage being unwound, not a forecast.
  • Bitcoin is about $57,850 early Saturday, down about 6.2% over 24 hours (CoinGecko). It fell below $60,000 on Friday for the first time since July and touched about $55,830 late Friday night ET, its lowest since September 2024, as worries about AI-stock valuations, higher borrowing costs and possible further Fed rate hikes hit risk assets; the Nasdaq dropped 3.8% Friday and US stocks were on track for their steepest weekly fall since April 2025 (Reuters via Investing.com, CoinDesk). About $1.5B to $1.6B of leveraged crypto bets were liquidated Friday, mostly longs (CoinGlass via CoinDesk, Crypto Briefing). The Crypto Fear and Greed Index reads 12, Extreme Fear (Alternative.me).
  • The XRP Ledger Foundation says the XRP Ledger is the first public, permissionless layer-1 blockchain to have its Architecture and Resiliency principles independently verified under the Global Layer One (GL1) Market Infrastructure Toolkit; CertiK did the verification in a report dated Nov 13 and announced it Nov 19 (XRPL Foundation, CertiK via GlobeNewswire). CertiK verified only those principles, not all ten; this is not a certification, regulatory approval or endorsement, and institutions still do their own due diligence (crypto.news, Crypto Briefing).
  • The XRP Ledger Foundation and VS1 Finance open-sourced a lending reference app on Nov 19 that combines five amendments (Credentials, Permissioned Domains, Single Asset Vault, Lending Protocol, Batch) on Devnet, the developer test network; native lending is still not live on the main ledger because the lending amendments have not reached the validator threshold (crypto.news).
  • Evernorth (Nasdaq: XRPN), the Ripple-backed XRP treasury company, reported cumulative write-downs of about $505M on its pre-listing XRP, carrying 346 million XRP at $342M at the end of September, nearly 60% below its original recorded value (about $2.44 per XRP on average) (CoinDesk, Nov 17, from its first quarterly report filed Nov 13). This is an accounting impairment that its rules do not let it reverse when prices recover; it does not mean Evernorth sold XRP. XRPN closed Monday down 5%.
  • US spot XRP ETFs had a net outflow of about $5M on Nov 20 (about $4.97M per SoSoValue via CoinDesk; $5.2M per CryptoETF.today), all from the Bitwise XRP fund, after two flat days. Total net assets slipped below $1B to about $990M, mostly because of the price drop; cumulative net inflows since launch are about $1.78B (CryptoETF.today, CoinDesk). November outflows of about $30.8M so far are close to March's record monthly outflow of about $31.2M (Bitcoin.com News). Separately, Roundhill withdrew its proposed XRP and Solana covered-call income ETFs on Nov 18 (CoinDesk).
  • About 281 million XRP (roughly $270M) moved from one holder into two wallets that are not identified as exchange deposit addresses, on Nov 6 and Nov 17; the receiving wallets were activated Oct 5 and tested with small transfers, which CoinDesk says is consistent with a planned custody move rather than a sale. The funding trail leads back to a Bitstamp-labeled wallet, which does not establish who controls the funds (CoinDesk, Nov 18, using XRPScan data). Separately, Binance's XRP futures open interest was about $325.7M on Nov 16, down about 81% from its July 2025 peak (CryptoQuant via The Crypto Basic), and a CryptoQuant contributor puts XRP's 30-day Binance liquidity index at a three-month low (crypto.news, Nov 18); thinner liquidity can mean sharper moves either way.
  • Ripple's Nov 16 Swell 2026 recap says the company expects to exit 2026 approaching a $900M annualized product revenue run rate with more than 1,700 institutions using Ripple (Ripple, Official; company figures, not audited). RLUSD supply was about $2.83B on Nov 20, up about 17% in a month (DefiLlama via Bitcoin.com News), and Binance is running a promotion from Nov 20 to Dec 18 that shares a 600,000 XRP pool among users holding RLUSD on Binance (Bitcoin.com News). RLUSD growth and exchange promotions are not the same thing as demand for XRP.
  • On the XRP Ledger, the proposed Single Asset Vault amendment had 25 of 35 trusted-validator votes and the Lending Protocol and its LendingProtocolV1_1 patch had 24 each as of Nov 20, each up two since Nov 17 (XRPScan via U.Today). Each needs 29 held for two straight weeks, so none is live and there is no activation date. Separately, XRPL developers will reset Testnet and Devnet from 8 to 9 p.m. ET on Nov 25 after a server failure, wiping test accounts; the main XRP Ledger and real XRP balances are not affected (CryptoPotato).
  • Ripple's Oct 27 partnerships still stand but are mostly plans: BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody, while direct RLUSD mint/redeem at BNY is subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph). Robinhood plans to use RLUSD on the XRP Ledger for institutional settlement through Bitstamp and to add RLUSD to its app later in 2026, with no start date or volume announced (Ripple, CryptoSlate). Separately, Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin supply has passed PayPal's PYUSD, about $2.80B vs $2.63B per DefiLlama, making it the seventh-largest stablecoin (Bitcoin.com News, Nov 16); RLUSD growth is not the same thing as demand for XRP.
  • At Ripple's Swell 2026 event, CEO Brad Garlinghouse said Ripple's large XRP holdings do not give it control of the XRP Ledger: Ripple runs one of the 35 validators on the recommended list, and holding XRP carries no voting weight (DailyCoin, Nov 10). Ripple CTO Emeritus David Schwartz disclosed on Nov 9 and 10 that his own holdings are down from about 26M XRP to about 3M over years of pre-arranged sales (crypto.news); that is a personal disclosure, not a Ripple sale.
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in new escrows, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold. The next release is due Dec 1.

How to read the labels

LabelMeaning
OfficialPublished by Ripple, a partner company, or a regulator about itself.
MainstreamGeneral financial press such as Reuters or Bloomberg.
Crypto pressEstablished crypto newsrooms such as CoinDesk, The Block, CryptoSlate.
CommunityCommentators, influencers and post summaries on X, blogs or video.
RumorA claim of fact or future event with no named source.
HighPrimary document, or a newsroom report with named sources or data.
MediumCredible outlet relaying third-party data or company plans not yet delivered.
LowPartly true, but the conclusion drawn goes beyond the evidence.
UnverifiedNo corroboration found. Treat as opinion.
ContradictedAvailable evidence points the other way.
