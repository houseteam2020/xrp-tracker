What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.20 early Friday, down about 9.3% over 24 hours and about 4.6% lower than 7 days ago, and about 15.8% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 3:36 AM MST; Kraken last trade $1.1987, 24h range $1.1761 to $1.3205). The broader crypto market also sold off overnight ahead of the US October jobs report due at 8:30 AM ET (6:30 AM MST).
  • Binance said on its official X account early Friday (about 2:08 AM MST) that the zero-knowledge proof for its latest Proof of Reserves is ready, so users can verify balances are backed at least 1:1 (Binance). That is the proof for the Nov 4 00:00 UTC snapshot covering 44 tokens. In Recovery Update #7 on Nov 4, Binance said about 98% of assets affected by the Oct 29 hack had been replenished from its own funds, with the remaining less-liquid tokens still being bought (Binance). Recovery Update #8 on Nov 5 reopened a third batch of withdrawals across six networks; Update #9 on Nov 6 continues more reopenings (Binance).
  • US spot XRP ETFs had no net flows on Thu Nov 5, a third flat day after Nov 3 and Nov 4, following about $529,510 of net outflows on Mon Nov 2 (SoSoValue via Crypto Radar Signals). Cumulative net inflows are about $1.81B; reported net assets about $1.41B on that dashboard.
  • The XRP Ledger's fixCleanup3_4_0 amendment went live Wednesday at 14:52 UTC (7:52 AM MST), at ledger 108,112,129 (XRPLWin via CoinDesk). It closes a flaw that, under certain conditions, let an account authorized only to burn an issuer's tokens also mint new ones. It affected issued tokens such as stablecoins, not XRP itself, and no exploitation has been publicly confirmed (CoinDesk).
  • Uphold launched RLUSD Earn on Nov 5, letting eligible customers earn a variable rate of up to 7% APY on Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin through the Uphold app via Morpho lending curated by Sentora; it is not a bank deposit and is not FDIC insured (Uphold via CCN, Nov 5).
  • crypto.news published a Nov 6 feature on Ripple and C2FO's supplier-financing plan that would settle Instant Pay on the XRP Ledger in RLUSD. Fees and reserves on the ledger still require XRP for operators, but the announced settlement currency is RLUSD, so invoice value alone does not imply proportional XRP demand (crypto.news, Nov 6).
  • On-exchange data: Binance's XRP reserves rose from about 2.35B to about 2.63B between Nov 3 and Nov 5 (about +280M XRP). The source of that increase (market buys, OTC, or internal moves) has not been confirmed by Binance (The Crypto Basic, CryptoQuant).
  • Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate in Tuesday's midterm elections (AP via Cointelegraph, Reuters). New members take office in January; analysts quoted by Reuters say a Democratic Congress may slow market-structure bills such as the stalled CLARITY Act.
  • Ripple and Coinbase announced Oct 28 that RLUSD can now be traded and held in the Coinbase app, Coinbase Exchange and Coinbase Prime, with 1:1 conversion from dollars, and deposits and withdrawals on the XRP Ledger, Base and Ethereum (Ripple, Oct 28). This is about the RLUSD stablecoin, not an XRP listing change.
  • Moody's rated RLUSD A2 long-term and P-1 short-term on Oct 27, citing reserves mostly in short-term US government assets and cash held at BNY, with a 103% coverage target; it also flagged that holders don't yet have a direct claim on reserves (Moody's, Decrypt, The Block).
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph).
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in three new escrows that open in June and July 2029, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold.
