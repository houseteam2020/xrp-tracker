What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $1.05 early Thursday, down about 0.7% over 24 hours, about 20% lower than 7 days ago and about 23% below 30 days ago (CoinGecko snapshot, 3:24 AM MST; $1.0538 last trade on Kraken (Kraken 24h range $1.0361 to $1.0795)). Wednesday's daily close was about $1.05, near the 60-session low of about $1.03 set Tuesday. XRP trades below its 50-, 100- and 200-day averages (about $1.27, $1.29 and $1.35), and the daily RSI momentum gauge is near 23 to 24, in oversold territory, which does not by itself signal a bottom (FXStreet). Analysts are watching $1.00 as the main support level; that is opinion, not a forecast.
  • Bitcoin held near $73,000 to $74,000 on Wednesday after Tuesday's hotter-than-expected October CPI, even as stocks rebounded on CoreWeave earnings; CME FedWatch put the odds of a quarter-point Fed hike in December near 90% (CoinDesk). US spot bitcoin ETFs had a fourth straight day of outflows ($95M on Tuesday), crypto futures liquidations jumped to about $725M in 24 hours, and XRP open interest fell 3.8% against a 1.8% price drop, a sign of leveraged money leaving rather than new shorts (CoinGlass via CoinDesk).
  • US spot XRP ETFs had no net flows on Wed Nov 11, after outflows of about $10.4M on Nov 9 and $7.6M on Nov 10 (SoSoValue via FXStreet, CryptoPotato). The last day with net inflows was Oct 29. Cumulative net inflows are about $1.8B and net assets about $1.2B.
  • On the XRP Ledger, the proposed native lending amendment has 22 of 35 trusted-validator votes, up from 19 in mid-October, and the SingleAssetVault amendment it depends on has 23; each needs 29 held for two straight weeks, so neither is live and there is no activation date (CoinDesk, Nov 12). The design offers fixed-term loans with no borrower collateral, relying on off-chain underwriting by loan brokers. Separately, XRPL developers will reset Testnet and Devnet on Nov 25, wiping test accounts; the main XRP Ledger is not affected (CryptoPotato).
  • At Ripple's Swell 2026 event, CEO Brad Garlinghouse said Ripple's large XRP holdings do not give it control of the XRP Ledger: Ripple runs one of the 35 validators on the recommended list, and holding XRP carries no voting weight (DailyCoin, Nov 10). Ripple does control the timing of its own escrow releases.
  • XRP Ledger payment volume jumped about 207% to about 1.16B XRP on Nov 9, roughly double its 30-day average (XRPScan data via TokenPost); that counts XRP moved in payments, including exchange transfers, and is not a measure of buying. Santiment counts about 343,500 wallets holding at least 10,000 XRP, up about 16,800 (5.2%) since Feb 9 (via Bitcoin.com News); wallet counts can shift with transfers and custody changes.
  • Ripple CTO Emeritus David Schwartz said in X posts on Nov 9 and 10 that his holdings are down from a high of about 26M XRP to about 3M, from about 1,500 BTC to 1, and from about 40,000 ETH to 6; he sold about 2.5M XRP in 2025. He cited his own discomfort with risk, said sales are pre-arranged in writing with an attorney at least 30 days ahead, and still holds Ripple stock. He also told frustrated holders there is 'no secret plan' (crypto.news, Coinpedia). These are personal sales over years, not a new Ripple sale.
  • Binance reopened withdrawals for HYPE and APT (TokenPost, Nov 9) and is targeting about Fri Nov 13 for its remaining paused tokens (INJ, PLASMA, NEAR, LTC) and all tokens on already-restored networks; that is a target, not a guarantee (Binance Recovery Update #9 via MABOnChain). XRP withdrawals on the XRP Ledger reopened Nov 3 and XRP on Ethereum on Nov 6.
  • BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody: Ripple and BNY announced Oct 27 that BNY's Digital Asset Custody platform now supports XRP and RLUSD for institutional clients. Plans to let BNY clients mint and redeem RLUSD directly, and a tokenized trade finance pilot with C2FO on the XRP Ledger, are still subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph).
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in three new escrows that open in June and July 2029, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold.
