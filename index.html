What's actually confirmed this week

  • XRP is about $0.955 early Tuesday, roughly flat over 24 hours (CoinGecko, about +0.4% to +0.7% depending on the endpoint) and about 12% lower than 7 days ago (CoinGecko rolling 7-day change; $0.9544 last trade on Kraken, Kraken 24h range $0.9418 to $0.9765). It closed Monday at about $0.955 (CoinGecko daily close), still above Saturday's low of about $0.89, its lowest since November 2024. It is down about 26% over 30 days (CoinGecko). FXStreet's Tuesday note puts XRP well below its 50-, 100- and 200-day averages with RSI near 25, in oversold territory; oversold is not a signal that a bottom is in.
  • Bitcoin is about $64,200 to $64,900 early Tuesday, still near the four-month low of about $62,000 hit over the weekend and down for six straight weeks (Investing.com, CoinGecko). US stocks fell Monday (Nasdaq −2.49%, S&P 500 −1.92%), and the Crypto Fear and Greed Index read 12 as of 00:00 UTC Nov 17 (KuCoin daily report). Markets are pricing a 25 or 50 basis point Fed hike in December (CME FedWatch via Investing.com). Strategy said Monday it bought 485 BTC for about $33M (Investing.com).
  • Evernorth (Nasdaq: XRPN), the Ripple-backed XRP treasury company, reported cumulative write-downs of about $505M on its pre-listing XRP, carrying 346 million XRP at $342M at the end of September, nearly 60% below its original recorded value (about $2.44 per XRP on average) (CoinDesk, Nov 17, from its first quarterly report filed Nov 13). This is an accounting impairment that its rules do not let it reverse when prices recover; it does not mean Evernorth sold XRP. XRPN closed Monday down 5%.
  • US spot XRP ETFs had a net outflow of about $19.13M for the week of Nov 9 to 13, the largest weekly outflow since March 2026 (TokenPost, Bitcoin.com News). Early Monday Nov 16 data is partial: CryptoKeyhole, which reads issuer holdings files, shows Canary's XRPC down about 5.2M XRP (about $5.0M) and 21Shares' TOXR flat, with Bitwise and Franklin still to report as of 05:35 UTC Nov 17; CryptoETF.today shows no net flow for Bitwise's XRP fund Monday. Cumulative net inflows since launch remain about $1.79B and combined net assets about $1.15B (TokenPost, as of Nov 15).
  • Binance's XRP futures open interest was about $325.7M on Nov 16, down about 81% from roughly $1.7B at its July 2025 peak and the lowest since November 2024 (CryptoQuant data via The Crypto Basic, Nov 17). On-chain analyst Darkfost says whale net outflows of XRP from Binance have exceeded $127M since Nov 10, about $21M on Monday alone (CoinNess, Nov 17); exchange outflows are often read as holding demand, but they do not show who moved the coins or why. Lower open interest means less leverage, not necessarily less selling.
  • Ripple's Nov 16 Swell 2026 recap says the company expects to exit 2026 approaching a $900M annualized product revenue run rate with more than 1,700 institutions using Ripple (Ripple, Official; company figures, not audited). It says the proposed XRPL Single Asset Vault and Lending Protocol remain subject to validator approval. RLUSD supply is about $2.80B, ahead of PayPal's PYUSD at $2.63B (DefiLlama via Bitcoin.com News, Nov 16).
  • On the XRP Ledger, the proposed native lending amendment has 22 of 35 trusted-validator votes and the SingleAssetVault amendment it depends on has 23; each needs 29 held for two straight weeks, so neither is live and there is no activation date (CoinDesk, Nov 12). Separately, XRPL developers will reset Testnet and Devnet from 8 to 9 p.m. ET on Nov 25 after a server failure, wiping test accounts; the main XRP Ledger, real XRP balances and transaction history are not affected (TokenPost, Nov 14).
  • Binance has reopened withdrawals on 87 networks, including LTC and NEAR, and says it plans to restore all remaining withdrawal services before next weekend (TokenPost, Nov 13). XRP withdrawals were not among the paused tokens in its latest list.
  • Ripple's Oct 27 partnerships still stand but are mostly plans: BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD custody, while direct RLUSD mint/redeem at BNY is subject to approvals (The Block, Cointelegraph). Robinhood plans to use RLUSD on the XRP Ledger for institutional settlement through Bitstamp and to add RLUSD to its app later in 2026, with no start date or volume announced (Ripple, CryptoSlate). Separately, Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin supply has passed PayPal's PYUSD, about $2.80B vs $2.63B per DefiLlama, making it the seventh-largest stablecoin (Bitcoin.com News, Nov 16); RLUSD growth is not the same thing as demand for XRP.
  • At Ripple's Swell 2026 event, CEO Brad Garlinghouse said Ripple's large XRP holdings do not give it control of the XRP Ledger: Ripple runs one of the 35 validators on the recommended list, and holding XRP carries no voting weight (DailyCoin, Nov 10). Ripple CTO Emeritus David Schwartz disclosed on Nov 9 and 10 that his own holdings are down from about 26M XRP to about 3M over years of pre-arranged sales (crypto.news); that is a personal disclosure, not a Ripple sale.
  • Ripple's November escrow cycle is complete: 1 billion XRP was released at 00:00 UTC Nov 1, and Ripple re-locked 700 million XRP in new escrows, leaving 300 million XRP outside escrow, the same pattern as September and October (TokenPost, Coinpaper, Whale Alert). That does not show the 300 million were sold. The next release is due Dec 1.

How to read the labels

LabelMeaning

MainstreamGeneral financial press such as Reuters or Bloomberg.
Crypto pressEstablished crypto newsrooms such as CoinDesk, The Block, CryptoSlate.
CommunityCommentators, influencers and post summaries on X, blogs or video.
RumorA claim of fact or future event with no named source.
HighPrimary document, or a newsroom report with named sources or data.
MediumCredible outlet relaying third-party data or company plans not yet delivered.
LowPartly true, but the conclusion drawn goes beyond the evidence.
UnverifiedNo corroboration found. Treat as opinion.
ContradictedAvailable evidence points the other way.
